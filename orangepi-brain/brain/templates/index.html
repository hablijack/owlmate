04 Places & navigation

Teach the owl a place (name + coordinates), then press Start — its head turns to point at that place, and keeps re-aiming as you walk. Say "bring mich nach <name>" (or press Stop) to end.

Pick a location
Click the map (or drag the marker) to set the coordinate.

05 System

Power

Sleep is policy from the brain; knocks and faces wake the owl on their own.

XIAO firmware update

Enter update mode to start the XIAO's isolated Wi-Fi access point. Then connect to RobotOwl-Update and open http://192.168.4.1/update to upload firmware.bin. Credentials are in the supervisor log. This does not upload firmware automatically. Tapping or walking will not enter or exit this mode.
